1. **P2, `build/bootstrap-tools.ps1:99`: Download bodies have no timeout.** `ResponseHeadersRead` ends the configured timeout once headers arrive; synchronous `CopyTo` has no separate deadline. A stalled CDN connection during a fresh bootstrap can therefore hang indefinitely while holding the tools mutex, blocking subsequent launches. Reproduce by interrupting body delivery after successful response headers without closing the connection. This follows [Microsoft’s documented timeout semantics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.net.http.httpcompletionoption?view=net-9.0). Apply cancellation to the complete download.
