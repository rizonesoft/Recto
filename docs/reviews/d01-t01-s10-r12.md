## Findings

1. **[High] Out-of-bounds read (`scopes[1]`) on unmatched closing tag at root scope (`src/pdf/document.cpp:584-592`):**
   - When `scopes.size() == 1` (only the root scope is active) and the XMP stream contains an unmatched closing tag such as `</bad>`, the loop `while (match_idx > 1u)` does not execute (`match_idx` remains `1`), and `if (scopes[match_idx].qname == close_qname)` reads `scopes[1]` out of bounds (`container-overflow` under AddressSanitizer).
   - **Fix:** Track `bool found_match = false;` inside the `while (match_idx > 1u)` loop and only pop scopes when `found_match` is true.

2. **[Medium] Quadratic scan in `decode_xml_entities` on inputs with many `&` and no `;` (`src/pdf/document.cpp:230`):**
   - `const size_t semi = text.find(';', i + 1u);` scans to the end of `text` on every `&` before checking `semi - i <= 10u`. On a malicious text node or attribute value containing tens of thousands of `&` characters without semicolons, this causes $O(N^2)$ scanning.
   - **Fix:** Bound the `;` search to a 10-byte window (`text.substr(i + 1u, std::min<size_t>(10u, text.size() - (i + 1u)))`) so every `&` inspects at most 10 characters ($O(1)$ per character).
