## Findings by Round

### Round 1 (`ef9f4b5df39c51cb2c387ac434ec01121c2e4055`) -- Verdict: `fixed`

- **High 1 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:425, 536, 589`):** Internal helper functions (`read_raw_token`, `read_comment`, `read_name`, `read_literal_string`, `read_hex_string`, `read_number_or_keyword`) were marked `noexcept`, preventing `std::bad_alloc` from reaching the `try/catch` boundary in `next_token()` and `peek_token()`.
  - **Fix (`a8a1653`):** Removed `noexcept` from internal allocating helpers so `next_token()` and `peek_token()` catch `std::bad_alloc` and return `ErrorCode::OutOfMemory`.
- **High 2 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:185`):** `position()` returned `lookahead_.front().byte_offset` after skipped whitespace, so calling `peek_token(1)` before consuming `stream` and calling `consume_stream_eol()` skipped the EOL immediately following `stream`.
  - **Fix (`a8a1653`):** Added `logical_cursor_` and `LookaheadEntry::cursor_after` so `position()` always reports the exact byte offset immediately after the last consumed token.
- **Medium 1 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:164`):** A failed `read_raw_token()` inside `peek_token()` advanced `cursor_` without rolling back, causing subsequent `next_token()` calls to skip the malformed token.
  - **Fix (`a8a1653`):** Saved and restored `cursor_` around speculative reads in `peek_token()`.
- **Medium 2 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:214`):** `consume_stream_eol()` accepted a bare `\r` without `\n`, whereas ISO 32000-1 section 7.3.8.1 forbids a single carriage return alone after `stream`.
  - **Fix (`a8a1653`):** Rejected bare `\r` after `stream` with `ErrorCode::DamagedFormat`.
- **Medium 3 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:522, 550, 574`):** Token byte ceilings were checked after `push_back` and omitted on odd-nibble padding in `read_hex_string`.
  - **Fix (`a8a1653`):** Checked `max_token_bytes_` before every byte append, including odd-nibble hex padding.

### Round 2 (`a8a1653d5eb4ee5e67345bd000ddfbea8809e6d4`) -- Verdict: `fixed`

- **High 1 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:109`, `src/pdf/lexer.h:167`):** `Lexer`'s `noexcept` constructor default-constructed `std::deque`, which allocates a debug container proxy in MSVC Debug (`/MDd`).
  - **Fix (`6d6cc57`, `7eff543`):** Replaced `std::deque` with a lazily allocated `std::unique_ptr<LookaheadEntry[]>` ring buffer and proxy-free `TokenText`, `TokenBytes`, and `PdfErrorMessage` containers.
- **Medium 1 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:179`):** If lookahead queue insertion threw `std::bad_alloc`, `cursor_` was not restored in the `catch` handler.
  - **Fix (`6d6cc57`):** Reserved the lookahead slot before reading the speculative token and restored `cursor_ = saved_cursor` in the `catch` handlers of `peek_token()` and `next_token()`.
- **Medium 2 (`src/pdf/lexer.cpp:168`):** Repeated `peek_token()` calls beyond EOF kept appending duplicate EOF entries and allocating.
  - **Fix (`6d6cc57`):** Reused the single cached `TokenKind::Eof` entry at the tail of the lookahead ring and bounded lookahead storage to `kMaxLexerLookahead = 64`.

### Round 3 (`6d6cc57e5471bc2e2148487559bc29531b9a5d65` -> `e6f27c75cdb972ab32d912de8cc34ac8dae30e14`) -- Verdict: `fixed`
