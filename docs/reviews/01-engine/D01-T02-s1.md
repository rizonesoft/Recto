The section is incomplete: two bounds defects can cut ink. Competitive superiority is also unproven. This is a static review of `93242fe`; I checked the numerical counterexamples with Python, but did not run the C++ suite or ASan. No files were modified.

**Part 1: completeness**

1. **present: Command recording and contiguous storage.** State, transforms, stroke styles, clips, paths, glyphs and images have recorded payloads in [display_list.h:379](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.h:379). Coverage: `PdfDisplayList.CommandRecordingAndContiguousStorage`, `PathRecording` and `DashPatternRecording`.

2. **present: Immutable ownership and concurrent reading.** Const storage, deleted copying and const accessors appear at [display_list.h:535](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.h:535). `OneShotFinishSemantics`, `DecodedBitmapZeroCopySharing`, `LiveDisplayListsCounter` and `ConcurrentRead` cover sealing, lifetime, shared images and eight readers walking 10,000 commands.

3. **present: Ordinary bounds, blank pages and crop-consumer metadata.** Coverage includes `ContentBounds`, `PageBoxConvention`, `CommandBoundsPerDraw`, `GlyphInkBoundsDescendersOverhangsAndSpaces`, `InvisibleContentHasNoBounds` and `ImageCoverageUsesMaskCells`. These establish useful coverage, but not the universal containment guarantee, as items 4 and 5 demonstrate.

4. **missing-in-scope: G1, bounds must include internal dash caps.** [display_list.cpp:1655](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.cpp:1655) computes the solid stroke’s bounds without dash information. That is not always a conservative envelope. For `(100,100) → (200,200) → (100,300)`, width `100`, projecting-square caps, bevel joins and dash `[140,1000]`, the first dash’s cap reaches **x ≈ 269.706**, while the reported solid-stroke maximum is **235.355**. Even adding 12 pt reaches only **247.355**, cutting approximately **22.35 pt** of ink. ISO requires caps at dash endpoints. Add this regression and transformed variants; preserve bounded work without expanding arbitrarily tiny dashes. Existing dash containment tests at [pdf_display_list_tests.cpp:1485](/R:/GitHub/Recto/tests/recto-tests/pdf_display_list_tests.cpp:1485) do not cover this geometry. [ISO 32000-1, §8.4.3.6](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/pdfstandards/PDF32000_2008.pdf)

5. **missing-in-scope: G2, finite cubic inputs must not silently produce underestimated bounds.** The extrema calculation at [display_list.cpp:289](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.cpp:289) allows intermediate overflow. X coordinates `[100, 1e200, -1e200, 100]` produce a NaN discriminant; both roots are skipped, leaving only endpoint bounds although the curve extends across the page. Input and transformed-point finiteness checks do not catch this. A crafted content stream can supply such finite coordinates. Require numerically safe extrema or a typed refusal before recording, with a regression covering finite inputs whose intermediate arithmetic overflows. `FiniteInputTransformationOverflowRejected` tests transformed-coordinate overflow, not this case.

6. **present: Typed malformed-input, allocation and resource-limit failures.** Validation and pool limits are implemented at [display_list.cpp:1234](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.cpp:1234). Coverage: `MalformedRecordsRefused`, `AccessorBoundaryValidation`, `SmallBudgetsRefuseBeforeAllocating`, `SaveDepthOverflowPreventsPublication` and `FinishControlBlockFailurePublishesNothing`. Item 5 remains an uncovered refusal path.

7. **missing-out-of-scope: G3, delivery through the real rendering and crop workflows.** The candidate alone cannot show a page or apply a crop. Interpreter, rasterizer, actor ownership, cancellation, partial-page recovery and shutdown remain assigned to **D01 T02 §§2–4, 6–10, 15–17, 19–21**; presentation belongs to **domain 02**; crop detection and application belong to **D05 T01 §4 / T02 §6**. These are existing planned owners, so duplicate sections are unnecessary. Completion requires their integration tests, including deterministic worker output, cancellation and crop containment.

Controls, menus, shortcuts, Ctrl+K entries, automation names, tooltips, dialogs, user settings, undo and persistence are not responsibilities of this `Surface: no` section. Its [Adjacency declaration:53](/R:/GitHub/Recto/todo/01-engine/TODO-02-actor-and-render.md:53) explicitly assigns presentation elsewhere. The buffer performs no file writes, so disk-full, locked-file, permission and restart read-back tests belong to the consuming workflows in item 7.

**Part 2: excellence**

**Competitor: Adobe Acrobat Pro.** It is the first reference under Recto’s parity policy. Adobe documents artwork-based **Remove White Margins**, page-range application, numeric page-box controls and margin reset. Its internal display-list implementation and comparative timings are not published in the sources inspected, so I cannot substantiate a strongest-engine ranking against Foxit, PDFelement or UPDF, or claim they outperform Recto on these specific inputs. [Adobe’s crop documentation](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/edit-documents/organize-pages/crop-pages.html)

1. **in-scope: G4, large-page recording has a quadratic-growth risk.** When geometric growth exceeds the byte budget, [reserve_growth:1274](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.cpp:1274) falls back to exact required capacity; [planned_capacity:786](/R:/GitHub/Recto/src/pdf/display_list.cpp:786) then permits another reallocation on every subsequent append. Dense pages approaching the budget can repeatedly copy large command and bounds arrays. The concurrency test pre-reserves its commands, so it misses this path. **Target:** demonstrate amortized linear recording work, including the budget boundary, with allocation/copy-count regression coverage and timings for dense pages. This is a concrete obstacle to beating competitors on time to first result, not a measured Acrobat timing comparison.

2. **in-scope: G1/G2, automatic crop safety is not yet dependable.** The two counterexamples above defeat Recto’s own stronger promise that automatic cropping never cuts ink. **Target:** zero containment failures for dash-cap and extreme-coordinate fixtures, including rotated/sheared variants; unsafe arithmetic must produce a typed error rather than successful understated bounds.

3. **out-of-scope: G5, end-to-end superiority needs measured evidence.** No replay or crop workflow exists in this candidate to compare speed, steps, recovery, accessibility or polish with Acrobat. **Target:** domain 06 benchmarks the same corpus and hardware against Acrobat, preserving Recto’s existing **<300 ms first-page**, **≤16.6 ms p95 cached scrolling**, and **<10 ms cancellation** targets; domains 02 and 05 verify keyboard-only operation, accessible feedback and crop step counts. Record actual competitor measurements and golden outputs before claiming a win. This needs downstream integration, not new controls in the command buffer.
