**Part 1: completeness**

1. **present: asynchronous opening and authentication.** `Runtime.OpenReturnsAtOnce` covers pending opening, `NeedsPassword`, authentication, missing files and cancellation during opening (`tests/recto-tests/runtime_tests.cpp:149`).

2. **present: admission failure and cleanup.** Actor-start and allocation failures return typed errors; unknown document IDs are refused. Evidence: `Runtime.AdmissionFailures`, `src/core/runtime.cpp:125`.

3. **present: error propagation for inaccessible inputs.** Actor opening forwards the engine error (`src/core/document_actor.cpp:479`); the file reader distinguishes missing files, access/sharing violations and other I/O failures (`src/pdf/document.cpp:1134`). Malformed-file interpretation remains the engine’s responsibility.

4. **present: tile routing, shutdown refusal and registration lifecycle.** Evidence: `src/core/runtime.cpp:203`, `Runtime.AfterShutdown`. Job cancellation, joining, unregistration and presentation-hook ordering are exercised.

5. **present: ordered teardown and repeated shutdown.** `Runtime.OrderedShutdownSequence` checks real job/presentation threads and live counters; `Runtime.IdempotentShutdown` checks explicit shutdown followed by destruction. `Actor.JoinWithHandlesHeld` covers retained handles.

6. **missing-in-scope: nonblocking document close for the shell.** `close_document()` calls `handle->join()` synchronously (`src/core/runtime.cpp:199`). A tab-close handler therefore waits on parsing, compilation or file I/O, contrary to foundation §4.3 and this TODO file’s Outcome. Provide asynchronous close completion while retaining ownership until joining finishes. Test that close returns while actor work remains pending.

7. **missing-in-scope: cancellation during an outstanding operating-system read.** `ReadFile` is synchronous, with cancellation checked only before entering it (`src/pdf/document.cpp:1189–1195`); closing then joins that actor. A stalled network share or storage device can therefore delay closing and quitting until Windows returns. `OpenReturnsAtOnce` holds a cooperative poll, not an outstanding read. Implement and test cancellation at the owning I/O layer.

8. **missing-in-scope: proof of shutdown under simultaneous active load.** The load test checks cumulative compile/render counters (`tests/recto-tests/runtime_tests.cpp:387`). Those counters remain positive after work finishes and do not establish four actors compiling and eight workers rendering at shutdown. Its tiles are also only 64 × 64 (`:101`). Synchronize active work at teardown, exercise production-size tiles, and cover destruction directly as well as explicit shutdown.

9. **missing-in-scope: the promised raster cancellation bound.** The candidate itself records cancellation exceeding 10 ms in 11 of 40 runs on a half-busy host (`todo/01-engine/TODO-02-actor-and-render.md:1713`). Filing §50 identifies the owning fix but does not fulfil this file’s cancellation Goal or §10’s reliance on bounded worker shutdown.

10. **present: persistence remains outside this coordinator.** The Adjacency rationale explicitly assigns no persistent records here (`todo/01-engine/TODO-02-actor-and-render.md:55`). Existing actor saves use the atomic-write layer; `Actor.SaveIncremental` and `AtomicWrite.CancelDuringReadBack` cover saving and cancelled read-back. Runtime needs no separate autosave store or full-disk policy.

**Part 2: excellence**

**Comparator: Adobe Acrobat Pro.** Adobe documents protected operation for hostile PDFs and temporary autosave files for recovering changes after interruption. These establish product behaviours, not its internal thread lifecycle or a guaranteed close latency. No equivalent measured comparison establishes Foxit, PDFelement or UPDF as stronger for this particular job. [Protected Mode](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/kb/protected-mode-troubleshooting.html), [saving and recovery](https://helpx.adobe.com/ca/acrobat/using/saving-pdfs.html).

1. **in-scope: closing is not inherently responsive.** The synchronous join makes responsiveness depend on the remaining engine work. Target: zero engine waits on the UI thread; a close request returns while a deliberately held actor remains pending. This repeats completeness item 6.

Cancellation needs to remain effective through I/O admission and completion without requiring application shutdown.

[`CancelSynchronousIo`](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/ioapiset/nf-ioapiset-cancelsynchronousio) cancels pending operations, not operations started afterward. Microsoft explicitly identifies cancellation between synchronous calls as requiring additional coordination in its [cancellation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/canceling-pending-i-o-operations).
