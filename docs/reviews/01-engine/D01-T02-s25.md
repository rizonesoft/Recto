## Self-review (implementing session, before the completion review)

- Failure modes named for this area (`AGENTS.md`): document bytes reaching the ring or ETW, and a misreported failure. `DecoderStep` is a closed `uint16_t` catalogue with a `constexpr noexcept` name table; `PdfError` and `DiagnosticEntry` carry `decoder_step`, `platform_status` (the HRESULT) and the same pair for an earlier attempt (`prior_step`, `prior_status`), as numbers only.
- Freeze check (`TODO-02` is `frozen: true`): the fields are appended after `bound`; the ETW fields are inserted before `ThreadId`, which stays last; no existing field or numeric value changes (the pinned field list and the name tables); no threading change; the capture parser's field limit went from 20 to 28 for the 25-field event.
- `wic_failure` sets the step and HRESULT for every WIC call site (16 in DCT and JPX, each mapped to its step); JPX remembers which step stopped WIC; a native failure after a WIC data failure is `NativeJpx` with WIC's step and HRESULT as its prior pair.
- `PdfError.DecoderContextReachesRingAndEtw` injects `E_OUTOFMEMORY` at the factory, decoder, frame and pixel-copy steps and reads each step and HRESULT back from the ring, its line and the captured packet, and the JPX hand-over's prior step and HRESULT from the ring entry.
- Decision taken (default, cost of reversing): stream and frame-information calls map to the nearest listed step (`CreateStream` and `InitializeFromMemory` to `WicCreateDecoder`, `GetSize` and `GetPixelFormat` to `WicGetFrame`, `CreateFormatConverter` to `WicConvertFormat`) rather than adding steps; appending a finer step later is one enumerator and its name.
