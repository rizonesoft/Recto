### adversarial: needs-attention

1. **P1, `src/pdf/interpreter.cpp:2041`: The page decode budget does not constrain cumulative filter-chain work.** Each stage receives the same remaining limit. Only after the entire chain finishes does line 2061 charge its work, saturating the counter and accepting the result even when it exceeded the budget. Reproduce by opening a PDF containing a 1×1 grayscale image with eight filters: Flate followed by seven RunLength stages. Construct the payload by wrapping 192 MiB of zeros in seven literal RunLength encodings, then Flate-compressing it. Every intermediate remains below 256 MiB, but the small compressed input causes over 1.5 GiB of decoded output before the 512 MiB page guard reacts. Enforce the remaining cumulative allowance during decoding.

### integration: needs-attention

2. **P1, `src/pdf/filters.cpp:11624`: The JPX handoff loses PDF Indexed sample values.** The native decoder scales sub-eight-bit samples to eight-bit intensities (`filters.cpp:10847`), while the interpreter treats those bytes as literal palette indices. Reproduce through the native JPX path with a two-bit, single-component codestream containing samples 1 and 2, and `/ColorSpace [/Indexed /DeviceRGB 3 <000000FF000000FF000000FF>]`. Both samples become values above 3 and clamp to palette entry 3: blue replaces the expected red and green. Preserve raw indices and their precision across this handoff.

### source-defect: needs-attention

3. **P1, `src/pdf/interpreter.cpp:2206`: JPX soft masks incorrectly apply `/Decode`.** `soft_mask_from` passes the array into `convert_samples` regardless of the filter. Reproduce by opening an image with a grayscale JPX `/SMask` containing samples 0 and 255, `/Decode [1 0]`, and absent or false `/ImageMask`. The recorded coverage becomes 255 and 0 instead of 0 and 255, reversing transparency on a valid file. ISO 32000-1 Table 89 requires readers to ignore `/Decode` for JPX images unless `ImageMask` is true. [ISO 32000-1](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/pdfstandards/PDF32000_2008.pdf)
