6. **missing-in-scope: memory-pressure recovery must preserve delivery, cancellation and worker availability.** Several paths violate the typed-error/exactly-once contract:

   - `finished` erases the entry before allocating its delivery record (`:331`); failure loses that completion permanently.
   - `next_job` returns no job after a tracking allocation failure (`:303`), permanently ending that worker.
   - `cancel_document` can abandon cancellation when its temporary vector allocation fails (`:523`, `:534`).
   
   Real memory pressure while displaying large documents is sufficient. Add scheduler allocation-failure coverage requiring no termination, one typed completion per accepted request, effective cancellation and a usable pool afterward. `CallbackAdmission` injects actor allocation failure, not these scheduler failures.

7. **missing-in-scope: shutdown must drain callbacks and avoid joining an actor under the scheduler lock.** Two real close paths remain unsafe:

Destruction can finish between those operations, allowing a delivery after destruction. Shared state protects internal memory, but does not protect the caller’s destroyed delivery target.
