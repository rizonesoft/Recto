- Freeze check (`TODO-02` is `frozen: true`): fields are appended to `PdfError` (`refusal_reason`, `observed`, `bound` after `generation_num`) and `DiagnosticEntry`, and the ETW fields are inserted before `ThreadId`, which stays last; no existing field or numeric value changes (`PdfError.ErrorCodeNamesCoverEveryCode` and the pinned field list in the ETW test); no threading change; `error_code_name` stays `constexpr noexcept`.

- The capture parser's field limit went from 12 to 20 (the `Diagnostic` event now has 17 fields), so the pinned field list proves the whole event.
