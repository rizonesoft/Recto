5. **missing-in-scope: One-pass traversal must actually retain only the small window.** The probabilistic doorkeeper treats hash collisions as repeat access and admits untouched objects directly to the main cache (`src/pdf/document.cpp:1667`, `:1713`). Reproducing its exact hash for 8,192 distinct generation-zero object numbers, 10 through 8,201, produced **151 false admissions**. With eligible 512 KiB values, those admissions can fill the main cache despite every object being read once. The 100-object test at `tests/recto-tests/pdf_document_tests.cpp:931` misses this. Require a long, collision-bearing traversal regression that preserves the 16-entry/1 MiB one-pass bound, including after doorkeeper resets.

6. **missing-in-scope: Prove the shared descendant’s individual parse bound.** `SharedObjectsParseOnce` asserts only that the entire page performs at most 116 parses (`tests/recto-tests/pdf_interpreter_tests.cpp:3182`). That does not establish its stated “each object at most twice” contract: repeated parsing of the descendant can fit inside the aggregate allowance. Count that object separately and require at most two parses for admission, then zero additional parses while resident. The regression must fail when descendant reuse alone is disabled.

7. **missing-in-scope: Allocation-failure coverage during eviction-triggering insertion.** The contract explicitly requires this, but `ObjectCacheAllocationFailure` constructs a fresh document and resolves one test object cold/window/main (`tests/recto-tests/pdf_document_tests.cpp:998`).
