## Findings by Round

### Round 1 (`0c0b47e306522ce966ca5749a87876a99814cbcc`) -- Verdict: `fixed`

- **P2 1 (`src/pdf/parser.cpp:564`, `src/pdf/object.cpp:376`):** `PdfDict::set` performed a linear scan over `entries_` on every insert, causing quadratic `O(N^2)` work on large dictionaries up to `kMaxContainerElements = 1,000,000`.
  - **Fix (`18a5552`):** Updated `PdfDict` in `src/pdf/object.h` and `src/pdf/object.cpp` to lazily build and maintain a transparent `std::unordered_map<std::string, size_t, TransparentStringHash, std::equal_to<>>` (`index_`) once `entries_.size() >= 32` (`kIndexThreshold`), providing `O(1)` amortized `set`, `get`, `get_mut`, `contains`, and swap-and-pop `erase` for large dictionaries while keeping small dictionaries (`< 32` entries) allocation-free.
- **P2 2 (`src/pdf/parser.cpp:634`):** The direct `/Length` fast path in `parse_stream_slice_impl` accepted a candidate `endstream` without verifying whether it was followed by `endobj` (when parsing an indirect object) or `endobj`/EOF, failing when a corrupted `/Length` landed directly on an embedded `endstream` token inside the stream payload.
  - **Fix (`18a5552`):** Updated `Parser::parse_stream_slice_impl` to take `bool require_endobj_terminator` and validate `is_endstream_followed_by_terminator(input, kw_pos, require_endobj_terminator)` in the direct-length fast path before accepting `kw_pos`, falling back to `strict_match` recovery and recording `DiagnosticId::DamagedStream` when `/Length` lands on an embedded `endstream` token.

### Round 2 (`18a55525e97dde917ccaf7eae675faf7ccc0c8fc`) -- Verdict: `fixed`

- **P2 1 (`src/pdf/object.h:264`, `src/pdf/object.cpp:523`):** `PdfDict` exposed mutable `begin()` and `end()` iterators over `std::pair<PdfName, PdfValue>`, allowing callers to mutate entry keys or reorder entries without updating `index_`.
  - **Fix (`f4ff59e`):** Made `PdfDict::iterator` alias `Container::const_iterator` and restricted `begin()` and `end()` to `const_iterator`, ensuring dictionary keys and entry order cannot be mutated outside `PdfDict` methods while preserving value mutation through `get_mut`, `get_array_mut`, `get_dict_mut`, `set`, `erase`, and `clear`.
- **P2 2 (`src/pdf/parser.cpp:683`, `src/pdf/parser.cpp:47`):** During fallback `endstream` recovery with missing `/Length`, each candidate `endstream` rescanned following `%` comments to find `endobj`, causing quadratic work on payloads with repeated `"endstream % "` comment chains.
  - **Fix (`f4ff59e`):** Added `TerminatorScanner` in `src/pdf/parser.cpp` to memoize the convergence offset (`cached_converge_limit` / `cached_tok_pos`) of EOL and comment-skipping scans so all candidate `endstream` checks across a stream recovery scan run in linear `O(N)` time.
