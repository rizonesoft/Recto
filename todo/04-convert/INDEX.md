# 04 Convert

> **Phases 4 and 5 (M4 Conversions A, M5 Conversions B)**

One job frame for every conversion, then the converters: PDF to images, images to PDF and PDF to text (M4), PDF to Word with its measured quality review and Office to PDF by COM or LibreOffice (M5). Honest conversion (SPEC principle 4) is this domain's standing rule.
